Address search

Works out of the box with free OpenStreetMap search. For the best US house-number matches, add a free key from Geoapify or LocationIQ (takes two minutes). Keys stay on this device.

Live aircraft relay

No free flight feed allows direct browser access, so aircraft needs a small relay of your own (free on Cloudflare). It can also carry address suggestions if your network blocks them.
